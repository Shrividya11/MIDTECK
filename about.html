About MIDTEK

A lighting company built around the LED.

MIDTEK focuses on one category — LED lighting — and works to get it right across the fixtures used in commercial, industrial and residential spaces.

What we do

LED fixtures, chosen with the space in mind

MIDTEK supplies LED lighting products designed for the way real spaces are used — from panel lights and downlights for interiors, to high bay fixtures for warehouses, to weatherproof luminaires for outdoor areas.

Every fixture we put forward is selected for consistent light output, energy efficiency and a working life that holds up under regular use.

How we work

Straightforward, from enquiry to install

We keep the process simple: understand the space, recommend the right fixture, and stand behind it with warranty coverage and support after the sale.

The current product range is listed on the Services page and is kept up to date as new fixtures are added.

What guides us

Principles behind every fixture

Efficiency

Lower power draw for the same or better light output, so running costs stay down over time.

Durability

Fixtures built to keep performing under continuous use in demanding environments.

Support

Clear warranty terms and a point of contact for questions before and after installation.
